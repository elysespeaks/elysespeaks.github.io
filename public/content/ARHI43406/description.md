### Topic
This seminar on contemporary art explores *the everyday*—theoretical, material, and historical—within recent artistic practice.  
We will look at artwork that:

* takes the everyday as source, subject, or material,
* adopts amateur or domestic materials and objects,
* mimics daily routines (eating, housekeeping, child‑rearing, etc.).

We will also examine theories of the everyday, routines, and habits.

### Goals and objectives  
After successfully completing this course you should be able to:

- **Describe** recent examples of contemporary artistic practices.  
- **Explain** how art reflects, responds to, and critiques notions of the everyday.  
- **Understand** historical and current intersections of art and everyday life.  
- **Look critically** and take an informed approach to visual subjects.  
- **Articulate** thoughtful, critical responses to visual material.  
- **Research & write** effectively on a visual subject.
