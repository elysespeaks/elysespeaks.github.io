## Requirements

* **Exams** – two exams  
  * **Exam 1:** 25 %  
  * **Exam 2:** 30 %

* **Preparation, participation, and attendance** – 20 %  
  > **Attendance**  
  > * One unexcused absence is allowed without penalty.  
  > * Each additional unexcused absence lowers the final grade one step (e.g., A → A‑).  
  > * More than **five** total absences (excused or unexcused) compromises the course’s learning goals.  
  >   
  > **Preparation**  
  > Coming to class is only the first step; you must **read** and **speak** to earn a high participation grade.  
  > Informed questions and comments enrich the discussion, and *there are no bad questions in this class*.

* **Presentation** – 15 %  
  * One 10‑minute presentation on an artist (schedule set during the second class).

* **Museum‑trip assignment** – 10 % (pass / fail)  
  * Short written piece on a work at the Raclin Murphy Museum.  
  * No late submissions without prior agreement or written excuse.

> *All assignments, exams, and papers are subject to the Academic Honor Code.*  
> As members of the Notre Dame community we pledge:  
> **“I will not participate in or tolerate academic dishonesty.”**  
> Unauthorized collaboration and generative‑AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Grammarly) are **not** permitted.
