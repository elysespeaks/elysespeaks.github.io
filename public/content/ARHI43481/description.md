### Topic  

In the wake of the post‑modern, theory‑heavy 1980s, many 1990s artists turned to **materially driven strategies** that challenged the conventions and power structures of the art world.  This seminar tracks three intertwined currents:

* **Embracing failure & amateurism** – adopting the aesthetics of hobby culture and Gen‑X disaffection.  
* **Institutional critique** – turning the museum’s own apparatus back on itself.  
* **The craft turn** – re‑valuing hand skills (embroidery, beadwork, weaving) to confront fine‑art hierarchies.

Artists we will study include **Mike Kelley, Cady Noland, David Hammons, Fred Wilson, Andrea Fraser, Renée Green, Cecilia Vicuña, Yinka Shonibare, and kimsooja**.

### Goals & Objectives  

After the course you should be able to  

* Describe recent examples of contemporary artistic practice.  
* Explain how material and institutional strategies challenge the art world.  
* Discuss key modes of contemporary art‑critical practice.  
* Look at visual material critically and articulate your thoughts.  
* Conduct basic research and write effectively on a visual subject.
